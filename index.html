Data sources

Coastal hazard and sea-level rise layers (FEMA flood zones, flooding potential, shoreline/cliff retreat, SLR inundation, tsunami hazard) are drawn from the Coastal Hazards & Sea-Level Rise Asset Vulnerability Assessment, completed by Western Carolina University at Golden Gate National Recreation Area, Presidio of San Francisco, and Fort Point National Historic Site. Exposure, sensitivity, and vulnerability scores in that assessment are reported alongside Facilities Management Software System (FMSS) data for each asset, along with intermediate indicator scores and full methodology in the source report.

Trail condition, replacement value, and deferred maintenance figures come from the PMIS/FMSS Asset Management Reporting System -- this public version shows those as relative tiers only, not exact dollar amounts or individual project titles/dates/funding sources. Wildlife observations are fetched live from the iNaturalist API. San Francisco encampment and service-request data is fetched live from the SF 311 API (data.sfgov.org). The full GGNRA trail network reference layer is fetched live from the NPS Public Trails dataset.

This is the public version of an internal tool. Individual project details, exact facility condition figures, and exact replacement/deferred-maintenance dollar amounts are intentionally not included in this build's data files.
